# Cloud Computing Assignment – IoT Optimization and AWS Architecture

This repository contains two components of an individual Cloud Computing assignment completed as part of the MSc program at Asia Pacific University of Technology and Innovation (APU), 2026.

## Part 1: Research Paper

### A Comprehensive Review of Optimizing Cloud-Based IoT Systems in Low-Connectivity Environments

This review paper explores current research on optimizing cloud-based Internet of Things (IoT) systems in rural and low-connectivity environments. It examines the challenges of intermittent network access, limited infrastructure, energy consumption, and inefficient resource utilization.

**Key areas covered:**
- Cloud resource optimization and intelligent task scheduling.
- Hybrid cloud-edge computing architectures.
- IoT data processing in low-bandwidth and offline environments.
- Energy-efficient and green computing strategies.
- Comparative analysis of existing frameworks and optimization approaches.
- Research gaps, unresolved challenges, and future directions.
- Applications in precision agriculture, healthcare, and environmental monitoring.

**Objective:** To critically review existing approaches and identify strategies for improving the reliability, efficiency, and sustainability of cloud-based IoT systems in rural environments.

## Part 2: AgriBio IoT Research Platform (AIRP) – AWS Cloud Architecture

This component proposes an AWS-based cloud infrastructure for AgriBio Labs, a provider of IoT solutions for rural agricultural, biological, and environmental research.

The architecture is designed to support distributed research operations in Kerala, India; Quebec, Canada; and Western Cape, South Africa.

**Key design areas:**
- Multi-tier IoT architecture integrating edge devices, cloud processing, and storage.
- Scalable IoT data ingestion and cloud-based analytics.
- Intelligent workload scheduling and resource optimization.
- Cost management through serverless processing and tiered storage.
- Security through encryption, role-based access control (RBAC), and audit logging.
- Data governance, integrity, and lineage tracking.
- High availability, backup, disaster recovery, and offline synchronization.
- Support for machine learning, predictive analytics, and research visualization.

**Objective:** To design a scalable, secure, reliable, and cost-effective AWS cloud architecture that supports rural IoT research despite intermittent connectivity and limited infrastructure.

## Technologies and Concepts

- **Cloud Platform:** Amazon Web Services (AWS)
- **IoT and Edge Computing:** MQTT, edge preprocessing, cloud-edge integration
- **Application Development:** Python, Django, Nginx
- **Data Storage:** PostgreSQL, MongoDB, Amazon S3
- **Machine Learning:** Scikit-learn, TensorFlow, PyTorch
- **Visualization:** Grafana, Plotly, Dash

*The technologies listed describe the proposed solution and technical scope; they do not imply that all services were deployed in a live AWS environment.*
